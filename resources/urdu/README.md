## Sir Dr M Sajid Khan

**[Mahad’s comprehensive notes](./sir-dr-m-sajid-khan/shaikh-mahad-notes/Urdu_Comprehensive_Notes_ShaikhMahad.pdf)** · [Set details](./sir-dr-m-sajid-khan/shaikh-mahad-notes/)

Notes by Shaikh Mahad Ud Din on literary genres, ghazal explanations, prose summaries, short questions, and poet reviews. The program, section, and term have not yet been recorded.

## Topics to look for

- **Literary genres:** Nasar, Dastan, Novel, Afsana, Drama, Mazmoon, Safar Nama, Ghazal, and Nazam.
- **Poetry:** Mir Taqi Mir, Mirza Ghalib, Hasrat Mohani, Faiz Ahmed Faiz, Nasir Kazmi, and Ada Jafri.
- **Prose:** Sair Doosre Dervish Ki, Kafan, Overcoat, and Jani Dushman.
- **Poet reviews:** Mirza Ghalib and Allama Iqbal.

Use a library or authorized copy for the assigned poems and prose themselves. This folder holds study notes, not scanned books.

For word meanings, consult the [Urdu Dictionary Board](https://udb.gov.pk/).
