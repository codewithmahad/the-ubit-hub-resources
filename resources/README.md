# Subject directory

Choose a subject to open its guide. Each guide explains the files available and recommends where to start.

- **[Communication & Presentation Skills](./communication-and-presentation-skills/)**: course notes on business writing, presentations, and communication.
- **[Data Structures & Algorithms](./data-structures-and-algorithms/)**: The DSA Blueprint roadmap, practice links, and printable tracker.
- **[Digital Logic Design](./digital-logic-design/)**: exam guide, notes, MCQ practice, and a logic-gates reference sheet.
- **[Linear Algebra](./linear-algebra/)**: semester lecture notes from Miss Fozia Hanif’s course.
- **[Software Requirement Engineering](./software-requirement-engineering/)**: course outline, typed notes, and handwritten notes.
- **[Urdu](./urdu/)**: comprehensive notes with poetry explanations and prose summaries.

Open a PDF to read it in your browser or download it for later. Compare the available material with your current course outline.

To add resources or a new subject, see the [contributing guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md). Upload only your own work or material with documented redistribution permission. Use library or authorized copies for textbook reading.
